cellhippo: three-modality tile clustering (H&E + expression + annotation)

Rebuilds HIPPO's phenotype chain with the H&E image actually in the feature
vector, alongside the Xenium expression matrix and the cell-type annotation the
original never had. The unit is the tissue tile, as in HIPPO, so the output is a
phenotype atlas plus a map of where each unit sits on the section.

Imaging conventions follow the reference implementations rather than being
invented: 224 px tiles, a tissue-coverage filter, Reinhard normalisation and a
contrastively trained encoder from ZhoulabCPH/HIPOS; the aligned-H&E + nuclei-mask
+ expression input contract from SydneyBioX/GHIST_plus.

Registration was the hard part and is solved from evidence rather than assumed.
he_imagealignment.csv as shipped disagrees with the OME metadata on the same TIFF
by a factor of 2.84. Rotation is taken from the matrix (second-moment matching
reproduces it within 4 degrees on both slides), scale from cell size (11.4 um
cells measured as 12.1 um at the OME pixel size; 34 um at the matrix's), and
translation from second-moment matching -- the objectives that failed first, IoU
and "fraction on tissue", both reward shrinking the cloud and slid to the search
boundary.

Results: 2,086 tiles from two slides, 64 fine clusters, 5 phenotypes that read as
anatomical domains (tumour nests, two grades of fibrous stroma, a mixed
stroma+lymphocyte+haemorrhage region, and gland-forming epithelium). The spatial
map shows strong autocorrelation, so the phenotypes are regions rather than noise.

Two slides is not a cohort, these datasets carry no survival follow-up, and G is
whatever BIC picks rather than a result -- all stated in the page.
